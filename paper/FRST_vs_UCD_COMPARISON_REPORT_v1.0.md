# FRST vs Unified Coherence Dynamics — Comparison Report v1.0

**Freeze/comparison date:** 2026-09-11  
**Scope:** compare the independently frozen Finite Relational Selection Theory (FRST) with Unified Coherence Dynamics (UCD) without silently merging either theory.

## Executive result

FRST and UCD overlap strongly at the level of **relational architecture**, but they are not the same theory.

FRST begins from a general mathematical object:

`R_FRST = (K, H_raw, P_phys, A_phys, H, rho_0, observables)`

with a finite primitive carrier and the hierarchy

`mathematical possibility -> physical admissibility -> realized state/history -> observable reality`.

UCD is much more specific. Its developed structures include the registration group

`Q_72 = Z_3 x Dic_6`,

and the local operator space

`H_504 = V_7 tensor C[Q_72]`,

with `dim(H_504)=7 x 72 = 504` and the developed 84+420 sector structure.

The comparison therefore has a simple direction:

**FRST supplies a generic relational possibility architecture; UCD supplies a specific candidate realization and asks whether that realization can be forced from deeper source law.**

## 1. Possibility, admissibility and actuality

The generic comparison hierarchy is

`Omega_realized subseteq Omega_physical subseteq Omega_mathematical`.

A convenient Hilbert-space form is

`H_phys = P_phys H_raw`,

where `P_phys` is the orthogonal projector onto the common kernel/intersection of the physical consistency constraints.

The physically admissible state space is not the same thing as the actual state. The actual realized state/history requires additional data, schematically `rho_0`, an actualization law, or an equivalent global boundary condition.

This separates three questions that are often conflated:

1. What can be written mathematically?
2. What satisfies the physical law?
3. Which permitted state/history is actually realized?

## 2. Where FRST and UCD structurally agree

Both frameworks naturally organize physics around relational states rather than treating particles, fields, geometry and time as independent primitive substances.

Common structural themes include:

- physical states selected from a larger mathematical space;
- relational rather than label-dependent observables;
- local-frame/gauge consistency;
- closed-loop/holonomy information;
- a lawful Hamiltonian or equivalent dynamical generator;
- spectral excitation costs as a natural route to mass/gap structure;
- emergent or effective geometry from relational data;
- continuum mathematics as an effective description rather than automatically an ontological infinity;
- quantum-to-classical organization through stable records/observable sectors.

These similarities are architectural. They do not prove that FRST and UCD are mathematically equivalent.

## 3. Where UCD is more specific

FRST v1.0 does not independently derive the numbers 7, 72 or 504, nor the group `Q_72`, nor the H84/H420 split, nor the H-net construction.

UCD contains those specific candidate structures. In particular,

`Q_72 = Z_3 x Dic_6`,

`H_504 = V_7 tensor C[Q_72]`,

`Delta_504 = 2 kappa exp(-1/72)`.

That number is a theorem of the defined finite H504/ring subsystem. It is not, by itself, the continuum Yang-Mills mass gap and it is not a theorem that the complete physical H-net is finite.

## 4. The critical non-import rule

FRST v1.0 starts with the axiom

`0 < |V| = N < infinity`.

UCD cannot simply inherit this as a theorem.

The UCD finite-realization branch is trying to establish why physically realized registration is finite from upstream UCD source principles. Therefore the comparison enforces:

`FRST finite actuality axiom != UCD finite-realization theorem`.

This is the most important firewall in the comparison.

## 5. Completed creation is not yet a finiteness proof

A completed set may still be mathematically infinite. Therefore the statement that creation is finished does not, by itself, imply finite cardinality.

The UCD v14 finite-realization work isolated the missing source-level route. If the realized actualization structure is a rooted tree with finite roots, finite branching, and no infinite realized strict branch, then the realized tree is finite by Koenig's infinity lemma.

The two remaining upstream obligations are therefore:

- `P0-F1`: finite immediate lawful actualization / finite branching;
- `P0-F2`: no infinite physically realized strict differentiation chain / well-foundedness.

The implication `finite roots + F1 + F2 => finite realized tree` is closed mathematics. F1 and F2 remain source theorems to derive inside UCD.

## 6. Selector theorem

FRST makes the selector explicit as `P_phys`. UCD already contains several physical-selection ingredients separately: registration, gauge, holonomy, provenance/subsystem structure and physical-sector constraints.

A useful UCD-level organizational definition is therefore

`H_UCD^phys = intersection_alpha ker(C_alpha)`

and

`P_UCD = Proj(H_UCD^phys)`.

This definition collects existing constraints under one operator umbrella. It does not prove that the full UCD selector has been uniquely derived.

The deeper target is a source theorem of the form

`Source law => unique admissible selector => Q72/H504/H-net architecture`.

That theorem is open.

## 8. Continuum versus physical infinity

The comparison suggests a clean distinction:

- **finite physical ontology:** a finite set/network of actually realized primitive registrations;
- **continuum or infinite mathematical envelope:** a calculational/approximation structure used to express large-scale or limiting behavior.

A finite physical theory may still reproduce local continuum physics to high precision. If UCD establishes finite physical realization, older continuum constructions should therefore be interpreted carefully as effective or verification structures unless a stronger ontological claim is independently proved.
