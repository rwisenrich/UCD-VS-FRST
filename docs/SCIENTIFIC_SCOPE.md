# Scientific scope of UCD VS FRST

This repository is a controlled comparison, not a merger of two theories.

## FRST

Finite Relational Selection Theory begins from a generic finite relational quantum architecture. It asks how a finite carrier, physical constraints, lawful dynamics and one realized state/history can give rise to geometry, fields, matter, mass, probability, records and effective continuum behavior.

Its central architecture is schematically:

`R_FRST = (K, H_raw, P_phys, A_phys, H, rho_0, observables)`.

FRST v1.0 assumes finite actuality.

## UCD

Unified Coherence Dynamics is more specific. Its developed structures include the Q72 registration carrier and H504 sector

`H_504 = V_7 tensor C[Q_72]`,

with `Q_72 = Z_3 x Dic_6` and `dim(H_504)=504`.

The comparison question is not whether UCD can be rewritten as FRST. It is whether the generic relational questions exposed independently by FRST help identify missing UCD derivations while preserving UCD's specific source/provenance chain.

## Primary research questions

1. What selects physically admissible states from the larger mathematical possibility space?
2. What distinguishes admissibility from actual realization?
3. Can finite physical realization be derived rather than assumed?
4. What source law forces finite branching and well-founded realized differentiation?
5. What forces the UCD-specific Q72/H504/H-net selector rather than another relational architecture?
6. How does a finite physical ontology produce controlled continuum approximations without treating physical infinity as fundamental?

Every result is labeled as theorem, conditional theorem, exact countermodel, numerical check, reconciliation obligation or open theorem.
